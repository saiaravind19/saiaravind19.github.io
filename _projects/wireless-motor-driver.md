## What it does

Runs in access-point mode. Connect to its WiFi network, open `wirelessmd.local`, and drive the motors from a browser UI. It also works as a conventional motor driver.

## Specs

- Custom PCB designed in KiCad 6.0
- 6–12 V supply, 1.2 A per channel, 500 mA fuse protection
